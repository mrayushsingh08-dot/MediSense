About DocMeet

Technology That Makes Healthcare Simpler

DocMeet is a modern healthcare platform designed to make doctor consultations more accessible, convenient and user-friendly. It brings patients and doctors together through a single digital platform.

From finding the right doctor to managing consultations, appointments and online healthcare services, DocMeet focuses on creating a smooth digital healthcare experience.

What DocMeet Offers

The platform provides different features for both patients and doctors to make the consultation process easier.

01

Doctor Discovery

Patients can explore verified doctors and find healthcare professionals according to their required specialty.

02

Online Consultation

DocMeet supports digital doctor-patient consultations, making healthcare interaction possible without requiring a physical visit.

03

Appointment Management

Users can manage consultation-related appointments through a centralized digital platform.

04

Secure Authentication

User authentication and role-based access help provide different experiences for patients, doctors and administrators.

05

Doctor Verification

Doctor onboarding includes a verification process so that approved doctors can become available to patients.

06

Digital Healthcare Experience

The platform brings multiple healthcare activities together in one modern and easy-to-use environment.

How DocMeet Works

A simple digital flow designed to make healthcare consultation easier for users.

1

Create an Account
